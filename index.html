UoS SafeCall

Campus emergency help, one tap away. One button connects students to Rescue 1122, Police, Campus Security and family, and shares their live location.

Open the live app Figma prototype
WHY SAFECALL

Built for students, by students

In an emergency, nobody wants to search for numbers or explain where they are. SafeCall does both in seconds.

One-tap SOS

A 5-second countdown, then Rescue 1122 is called automatically.

Live campus location

Real GPS, the campus map and the nearest hospitals and police.

Verified numbers

Rescue 1122, Police 15, Fire 16, Edhi 115 and campus contacts.

Trusted contacts

Family gets an SMS or WhatsApp with your live map link.

THE APP

Five screens, one simple flow

Designed in Figma, then built as a real web app that installs on any phone.

HOW IT WORKS

Help in four steps

Press SOS

One big button on the home screen. No menus, no typing.

Location is found

The phone's GPS finds you and checks if you are on campus.

Contacts are alerted

An SMS or WhatsApp with your map link is ready to send.

1122 is called

After 5 seconds Rescue 1122 is dialled, unless you cancel.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

A UI/UX project at the University of Sargodha

UoS SafeCall was designed in Figma and built as a working web app for our UI/UX Design exhibition. It shows how good design can make emergency help faster and simpler.
