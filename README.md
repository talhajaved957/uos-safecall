# UoS SafeCall

Campus emergency help for University of Sargodha students, one tap away.
This is a UI/UX Design project. It was designed in Figma and then built as a working web app.

- **Website:** https://talhajaved957.github.io/uos-safecall/
- **Live app:** https://talhajaved957.github.io/uos-safecall/app.html
- **Figma prototype:** https://www.figma.com/proto/uasB5fXJMMcS6ubk2ZSvzR/UoS-SafeCall?node-id=2-2&starting-point-node-id=2%3A2&scaling=scale-down

## What the app does
- One-tap **SOS**: a 5-second countdown, then Rescue 1122 is called
- **Live GPS location**: shows when you are on the University of Sargodha campus
- **Emergency contacts**: Rescue 1122, Police 15, Fire 16, Edhi 115, Motorway 130, campus contacts and family
- **SMS / WhatsApp** alert with a Google Maps link to your location
- **Campus map** (OpenStreetMap), plus the nearest hospitals and police
- Installs on the phone home screen and works offline (PWA)

Your saved data stays only on your own phone (browser storage).

> Student project. In a real emergency, always call 1122 directly if the app does not work.
